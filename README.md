### Problem Statement
This project analyzes global university rankings based on various elements.
The Usecase questions are:
- The top 10 universities globally.
- The top 10 universities for employment outcomes.
- The position of Saudi Arabian universities in global rankings.
- The most significant factors influencing a university's overall ranking.
- The correlation between national and global rankings to identify countries with a high concentration of top universities.

## Dataset Overview
The data used are sourced from Kaggle:
